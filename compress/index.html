Compress a photo under an upload limit

Online forms refuse photos over their size limit: 240 KB for the DS-160, 200 KB for OCI, 20 KB for a PAN card. This shrinks the file to fit, keeping the shape, and saves it as JPEG. Nothing is uploaded.
