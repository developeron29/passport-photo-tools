HEIC to JPG converter

iPhones save photos as HEIC, and most passport, visa and exam forms only accept JPEG. Convert one or more HEIC photos to JPG here. The conversion runs in your browser, so your photos stay on your device.

Avoid HEIC next time

On iPhone, go to Settings, Camera, Formats and choose Most Compatible. New photos are then saved as JPEG.
