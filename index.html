Free passport photo tools

Three small tools for the parts of a passport or visa photo that usually go wrong. They run entirely in your browser: your photo is never uploaded. The code is open source.

Photo checker

Check pixel size, shape, file size, format, background and head position against the rules for 25 documents, including the DS-160, UK online passport and OCI.

Compress to a size limit

Get a photo under 240 KB (DS-160), 200 KB (OCI), 20 KB (PAN) or any limit, without changing its shape.

4x6 print sheet

Tile a 2x2 inch or 35x45 mm photo onto a 4x6 inch sheet with cut lines, ready to print at any photo counter.

Need the whole photo made for you? visaphotos.app crops, sizes and checks a photo for 151 passport, visa and ID documents, with rules taken from official sources. Free preview; the download is paid.
