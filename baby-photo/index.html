Baby and child passport photo checker

Taking a baby's passport photo at home is hard. Add the photo and check it against the U.S. Department of State's rules for babies and children before you print or upload it. Nothing is uploaded.

    How to take it

    • Lay your baby on a plain white or off-white sheet, or cover a car seat with the sheet and sit them in it.
    • Light the face evenly, from a window in front of them, so there are no shadows.
    • No one else can be in the photo, including a parent's hands.
    • Your child must face the camera with a neutral expression and the whole face visible.
    • A baby's eyes don't have to be fully open. All other children must have their eyes open.

    Rules from the U.S. Department of State's passport photo page (Babies and toddlers). Checks are automatic estimates; the passport agency decides.
