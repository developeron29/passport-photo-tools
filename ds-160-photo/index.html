DS-160 photo fixer

The DS-160 refuses photos that aren't square, are under 600 x 600 px, are over 240 KB, or have the head the wrong size. Add a front-facing photo and this finds your face, crops it square with the head at the right size, and saves a JPEG that fits. Nothing is uploaded.

    Download DS-160 photo

    This crops and resizes only; it doesn't change your face or the background. A plain white or off-white background and no glasses are still required.

    DS-160 photo rules

    • Square JPEG, 600 x 600 to 1200 x 1200 px, 240 KB or less
    • Head 50–69% of the image height (chin to top of hair)
    • Plain white or off-white background, no glasses, taken in the last 6 months

    From the U.S. Department of State's photo and digital image requirements. If the upload still fails, the DS-160 FAQ says to bring one printed photo with your confirmation page to the interview.
