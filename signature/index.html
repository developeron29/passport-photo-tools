Signature resizer for online forms

Sign on plain white paper with a black pen, take a photo of it, and this crops to your signature, whitens the paper and saves a JPEG at the exact size and KB limit your form asks for. Nothing is uploaded.

Download JPEG

Tips

  • Sign in black ink on plain white paper, larger than you normally would. Fill a box about 6 x 2 cm.
  • Photograph it straight on in daylight, with no shadow from your phone.
  • Some forms (SBI PO, RRB) reject signatures in capital letters; sign the way you normally do.

Sizes and KB limits come from each exam's official notice, as tracked by visaphotos.app. Always check the current notice before you apply.
