    What this checks

    • Format: most portals want JPEG. PNG and HEIC (iPhone) files are often refused.
    • Shape and pixels: the photo must have the document's proportions (square for US and OCI photos) and enough pixels.
    • File size: online forms have hard limits, such as 240 KB for the DS-160 and 200 KB for OCI.
    • Background: plain and light, and white where the document asks for white (or not white, for OCI).
    • Head: one face, centred, level, and the right size for documents that publish a head-height range.

    Head and background checks are automatic estimates. The issuing authority makes the final decision.
